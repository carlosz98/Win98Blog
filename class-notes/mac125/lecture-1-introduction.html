Lecture 1: Introduction

C++ was created in 1983 by Danish computer scientist Bjarne Stroustrup, who wanted a language that was fast, simple to use, cross-platform and had high-level features.

  • C++ is great for handling lots of data, handling it efficiently, and doing it in a readable way.

  • It is still near the top of the TIOBE index (#2 in September 2024, behind Python).

  • Used in industry by Amazon, Meta, Apple, Google and game studios (Among Us was on the slide).

Likely test points: who created C++ and when (Stroustrup, 1983); why C++ is used (speed and efficiency with large data).
